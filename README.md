Projects:
<br>

[Multi-Fidelity Comparison](https://github.com/AngadKumar16/mf_comparision) How do you best combine lots of cheap, rough data with a little expensive, accurate data? I compare Gaussian processes, MF-DNNs, MF-KANs, and a KAN+DNN hybrid on Boston Harbor sea surface temperature. Submitted to JMLMC.

[Replicating Bhumbra & Beato (2013)](https://github.com/AngadKumar16/ReScience-C) We rebuilt their Bayesian Quantal Analysis from the paper alone, without the original code, then stress-tested it with simulation-based calibration and an MPFA baseline. Submitted to ReScience C.

[Coronary artery segmentation]((https://github.com/myalageri22/Automated_Cardiovascular_Segmentation_Bioprinting)) With [@myalageri22](https://github.com/myalageri22), we built a pipeline that turns CT scans into connected 3D vessel meshes clean enough for a bioprinter. Submitted to JIIM.

[GPClarity](https://github.com/AngadKumar16/GPClarity) lets you look inside Gaussian process models in GPy or Emukit: what the kernel is doing, where the uncertainty comes from, and which data points matter most. No changes to your training code.
